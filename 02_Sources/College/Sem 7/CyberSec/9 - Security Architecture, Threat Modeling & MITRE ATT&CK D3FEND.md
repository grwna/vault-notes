## Raw Notes
### Security Architecture
**Why architecture matters**
- Fixing flaw at design time is cheaper
- Most breaches are design meakness + bug

**Security by Design**
- Least privilege
- Fail-safe
- Complete mediation
- Separation of privilege
- Kerkhoffs principle

**Modern Design Principles**
- Defense in depth
- Zero trust
- Secure defaults and by default
- Assume/simulate breach
- Minimize attack surface

- Trust boundaries

### MITRE ATT&CK & D3FEND
- Knowledge base of adversary behaviors
- Organized as Tactics (why) and Techniques (how), does not suggest fixes.
- Community maintained

**D3FEND**
- Tactics
	- Model
	- Harden
	- Detect
	- Isolate
	- Deceive
	- Evict
	- Restore
- Techniques
	- MFA
	- Configuration Hardening
	- Inbound & Network Traffic Filtering
	- Process Spawn Analysis
