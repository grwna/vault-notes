## 1. Quick Summary 
-  Sorting algorithms generally use comparison and are bounded by the lower bound complexity of $O(n \text{ log }n)$. 
- Non-comparison sorting algorithms can achieve $O(n)$ time, but are limited to some types of problems
- Algorithms include: counting sort, radix sort, bucket sort.
