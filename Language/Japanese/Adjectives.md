いい - good, but conjugated as よい 
- negative --> よくない
- past --> よかった

Adjectives can be turned to Adverbs:
- i-adjectives, replace い with く(はやい -> はやく)

Adjectives can be turned to verbs (clean -> become clean):
- na-adjectives: (adjective + に + verb)
- i-adjectives: turn to adverb

You can connect an i-adjective (or a sentence ending on an i-adjective) "x" to another sentence "y" by replacing -i with -kute. The meaning becomes "x and y". This grammar part is commonly referred to as "te-form".

For na-adjectives, simply add 'de' (nothing is removed)
