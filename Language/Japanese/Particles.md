
- ぞ
	- "zo" is a sentence ending particle used by males. It adds force and is used o e.g. express strong intent or to persuade someone.
- よ ---> 頼むよ先生
	- yo adds emphasis. Someone asks the teacher for a favor. "I beg you, teacher". Note, that the person is directly addressing the teacher by his title ("sensei"), how to address people will be covered later in more detail.


Quick list

| **Particle**  | **Romaji**    | **Main Function**                              | **Example**                                         |
| ------------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **は**         | _wa_          | Topic marker                                   | 私は学生です (_I am a student_)                           |
| **が**         | _ga_          | Subject marker / Focus                         | 猫が好きです (_I like cats_)                              |
| **を**         | _o / wo_      | Direct object marker                           | 水を飲む (_Drink water_)                                |
| **に**         | _ni_          | Target, time, static location, indirect object | 7時に行く (_Go at 7 o'clock_) / 東京に住む (_Live in Tokyo_) |
| **で**         | _de_          | Action location, means/tool                    | バスで行く (_Go by bus_) / 店で食べる (_Eat at a shop_)       |
| **の**         | _no_          | Possession, noun connector, nominalizer        | 私の本 (_My book_)                                     |
| **へ**         | _e_           | Direction / Movement toward                    | 日本へ行く (_Go toward Japan_)                           |
| **と**         | _to_          | "And" (exhaustive list), "With" (together)     | 友達と話す (_Talk with a friend_)                        |
| **も**         | _mo_          | "Also", "Too"                                  | 私も行く (_I am also going_)                            |
| **から / まで**   | _kara / made_ | From / Until (time/space bounds)               | 9時から5時まで (_From 9 to 5_)                            |
| **や**         | _ya_          | "And" (non-exhaustive list)                    | りんごやみかん (_Apples, oranges, etc._)                   |
| **か**         | _ka_          | Question marker, "Or"                          | これですか？ (_Is it this?_) / 犬か猫 (_Dog or cat_)         |
| **よ / ね**<br> | _yo / ne_     | Sentence ending (emphasis / seeking agreement) | 美味しいね (_It's delicious, isn't it?_)                 |
**Note**
- は can be replaced with って

**Note**
"ni, he, de" can be combined with "ha, mo" to set the target or context as topic, here's [Tae Kim's explanation](http://www.guidetojapanese.org/learn/grammar/verbparticles#When_location_is_the_topic). Note that "wo" cannot be combined with these particles, so "fish wo mo tabeta" is wrong. You'd simply say: "fish mo tabeta".
