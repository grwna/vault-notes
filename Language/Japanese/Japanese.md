# Adjectives
## **na-adjectives**
na-adjectives and nouns are pretty much the same.
- noun + da declares the noun as state-of-being.
- noun + na acts as adjective, describing another noun.
- noun and na-adjectives are pretty much the same.

>"すてきだね"　-> noun
>"すてきな夢"    -> na-adjective

**Past Tense**
- Append だった

**Negative Form**
- Append じゃない (just like nouns)

**Negative Past Tense**
- Add じゃなかった
## **i-adjective**
Adjectives that ends in 'i', although some na-adjectives also ends in 'i'. Don't fret.
cannot be used as nouns like na-adjective

>"かわい\い\人"

**Past Tense**
- Remove an い, then add かった

**Negative Form**
- Remove an い, then add くない

**Negative Past Tense**
- Add じゃなかった

## Stem
The change from す to し.
Like in: 
- 話
- 話す


# Random
**Directions:**
- achira/acchi: distant from both speaker/listener
- sochira/socchi: distant from speaker, close to listener
- kochira/kocchi: close to speaker, far from listener
