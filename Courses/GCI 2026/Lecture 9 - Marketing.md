# Introduction
- Business: act of solving an issue and getting paid for it
- Good business needs good issue
	- Small issue -> small business
	- Big issue -> big business
- What makes a good issue? 
	- It is fundamental (roots), has a large impact
	- Comes with deep/suggestive hypothesis
	- You know it can be answered (not merely speculative)
- Balancing the three aspects is difficult (1 makes it larger, 3 narrows it down)
